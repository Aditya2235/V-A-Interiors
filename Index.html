Our Philosophy & Leadership

Our Story

Founder & Creative Director

Urvashi Sathaye

Our spaces define us and act as a natural extension of our personalities. They need to work intuitively and functionally while feeling welcoming and beautiful. This is where thoughtful design comes in—crafting spaces that make you happy, that are easy yet practical, and won't burn holes in your pocket.

With over a decade leading residential, office, and boutique commercial transformations across Mumbai and beyond, Urvashi combines spatial balance with tactile warmth, prioritizing how natural daylight and tailored joinery elevate the everyday human experience.

"Architecture is never about decorating four walls; it is about orchestrating human serenity."
B.Arch • Master of Spatial Design
Co-Founder & Managing Partner

Kabir Sathaye

Kabir directs structural detailing, engineering coordination, and precision on-site execution. His background in sustainable building techniques guarantees that bespoke woodwork, concealed MEP services, and complex acoustic finishes are delivered to international benchmarks.

Believing that execution integrity is what transforms poetic sketches into living heirlooms, he manages the entire lifecycle of contractor alignment, material testing, and strict milestone adherence so clients experience zero stress from foundation to handover.

"Execution integrity turns poetic concepts into living heirlooms that stand the test of time."
M.Eng Structural Systems • Project Fellow
